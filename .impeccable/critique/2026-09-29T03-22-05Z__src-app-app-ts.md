## Overall Impression

Entry is fast and the look holds in both themes (170 text pairs measured, one failure). Weak point: recovery does not survive real use. Undo disappears on the next tap, a second tab overwrites the first, landscape hides the list.

## What's Working

- Entry speed: Anotar opens focused, sheet above the keyboard, Enter submits.
- Settings warn before lowering the target ends the round.
- No target under 48x48, no unnamed element, focus never escapes an open sheet, reduced motion leaves zero animations.

## Priority Issues

1. [P1] The layout collapses when height is scarce. Landscape 844x390: score list height 0, totals clipped. Winner buttons below the fold at 320x568 with no cue. Fix: height-based breakpoint making the lockup a compact strip (drop min-height 172px); pin winner actions to the bottom with scores scrolling above. Files: src/app/components/team-lockup.ts, src/app/components/winner-modal.ts, src/app/app.css. Command: /impeccable adapt
2. [P1] The undo safety net is fragile. addPoints clears a pending undo; reload discards it; "Todo" wipes everything in one tap; two tabs do not sync and last write wins. Fix: keep undo until the next destructive action, persist it, rehydrate on the storage event. Files: src/app/game/game.store.ts, src/app/game/storage.ts, src/app/components/reset-sheet.ts. Command: /impeccable harden
3. [P1] A selected row hides what it deletes, and hands cannot be edited. Fix: keep the points chip visible in the selected state; add "Editar" that reopens the points sheet prefilled and replaces the row in place. Files: src/app/components/score-list.ts, src/app/components/points-sheet.ts, src/app/game/state.ts. Command: /impeccable shape
4. [P2] Screen-reader, keyboard and large-text gaps. Focus falls to body after deleting a hand and after Deshacer; on coarse pointers every sheet close blurs focus deliberately (sheet.ts onClose). Undo action focus ring is 1.00:1 on three of four sides. Points dialog is named "Anotar para" with no team. The win and a completed undo are not announced. Disabled "Guardar" label in settings is 1.41:1 dark, 1.14:1 light (.primary keeps --label: var(--c-ground)). At 320px with 200% text "Anotar" is clipped and the undo message is cut off. Team name input has outline: none. Files: src/app/components/sheet.ts, score-list.ts, undo-snackbar.ts, points-sheet.ts, settings-sheet.ts, winner-modal.ts, src/app/directives/fit-text.ts. Command: /impeccable audit
5. [P2] The app is silent about being installable and about updates. No beforeinstallprompt handling, no iOS hint, no SwUpdate. Fix: dismissible install line in the empty state when not standalone; "Nueva versión · Actualizar" only when no round is in progress. Files: src/app/app.ts, src/app/app.config.ts, src/app/components/score-list.ts. Command: /impeccable onboard

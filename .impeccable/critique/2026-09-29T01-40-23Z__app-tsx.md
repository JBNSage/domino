## Design Specificity Verdict

Design review: the structure is authored for this product; the skin is not. Two totals over a two-column ledger mirrors the paper score sheet, and the Spanish copy is how people at a table talk. The racing livery refers to nothing about dominoes. The user chose that look, so it is a trade rather than an error. Functional costs: the +30 buttons are the most button-like objects while the team panels (the main action) look like displays; small secondary data is set in 13pt extra-bold italic capitals.

Deterministic scan: detector ran, exit 0, 0 findings; it targets HTML and CSS so this says little about React Native. Source checks: all text pairs pass 4.5:1 at rest in both themes; no touch target below 48pt; all buttons have label and role; team B fill vs light ground 1.02:1 and team A 2.79:1 (3:1 needed); long press is the only route to the quick value and has no accessibility action (src/components/QuickAddBar.tsx); 16-character names truncate (src/components/TeamLockup.tsx, src/components/WinnerModal.tsx); delete control overlaps team B points at 320pt width or large font, estimated (src/components/ScoreList.tsx); 8 texts have no font-scaling limit.

Visual overlays: none, native app.

## Overall Impression

Legible, fast, loses nothing on interruption. Weak point is recovery from a mistake: a mistyped hand that crosses the target leads to a celebration whose only exit awards the round to the wrong team.

## What's Working

- Totals readable across the table: 76pt graphite numerals on saturated fills, identical in light and dark.
- The ledger lines up with the teams, so team is told by position as well as colour.
- Undo instead of confirmation for deleting, plus full persistence.

## Persona Red Flags

Casey (one-handed): top-left team panel is the hardest reach every hand; +30 sits just above the home indicator; screen is not kept awake so a phone on the table locks.

Sam (screen reader, large text): no VoiceOver announcements; TalkBack reads the backdrop as a full-screen "Cancelar" before the real one; team panel label omits "Faltan"; newest row marked only by a tone step of about 1.25:1; totals scale least (1.2x).

Jordan (first-timer): instruction is small muted text beside two loud +30 buttons; refresh icon reads as reload; the two +30 buttons are identical apart from colour; instruction disappears after the first hand.

## Minor Observations

- Hand number between two score columns can be misread as a score; filler "0" adds nothing.
- Deleting an early hand renumbers later ones.
- Undo bar covers the newest row and does not say which hand was removed.
- With 4-digit totals the two team numbers shrink independently.
- Typing "0" shows an error while the placeholder is also "0".
- "0 RONDAS" at the start is noise.
- Buttons shift 3pt when pressed with Reduce Motion on; first opening of the target sheet may still slide.
- expo-symbols is installed but unused.

## Questions to Consider

- If the bonus is occasional and hand entry is constant, why does the bonus own the best spot on the screen?
- Should the winner screen commit anything, or should the round close only when the next hand is recorded?
- The phone lies flat with players on all sides. Who is the scoreboard oriented for?
